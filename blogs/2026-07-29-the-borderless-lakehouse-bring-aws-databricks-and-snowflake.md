Today’s data lakehouse is no longer mere data repository, but increasingly a system of action, actively executing tasks via always-on, autonomous AI agents. Rather than waiting for static reports, these agents run continuous, real-time reasoning loops, monitoring supply chains, flagging anomalies, and executing business workflows. To scale this model, AI agents need to access your entire data estate and the right context to understand what data to use and when.

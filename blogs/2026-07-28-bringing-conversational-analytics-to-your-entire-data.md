Increasing the adoption of generative AI across the enterprise requires you to do more than deploy a generic chatbot with a custom wrapper. Interacting with business-critical databases demands absolute trust, strict governance, and deep grounding in enterprise semantics. Over the last year, Conversational Analytics (CA) in Google Cloud has moved from isolated experiments to scaled, enterprise-wide deployments.
